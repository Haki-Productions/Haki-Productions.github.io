        Objection responses

        Never lead with pricing
        "Too expensive."

        "What is the one thing you need most?" Then offer the smallest project that solves it.

        Never discount without scope reduction. Reduce scope, not quality.

        "I need to think about it."

        "What is the specific thing you are unsure about?" Isolate the real objection and answer it.

        Never say "no problem, just let me know."

        "Can you do it cheaper?"

        "I can reduce scope, not quality. Here is what we cut." Name the exact trade.

        Never say "maybe" or "we'll see."

        "I found someone cheaper."

        "Good. Compare what they deliver for that price. If they do the same thing for less, take it. If they do less, you are not comparing the same thing."

        Never get defensive or start a price war.
