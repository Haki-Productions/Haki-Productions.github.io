Case 01 / In-house label

Soul Terror

In-house Identity Apparel Campaign Lookbook

The brief

Build a streetwear label that feels like it came from the street, not a moodboard. One identity strong enough to carry apparel, campaign photography and a lookbook without falling apart.

What we made

A full identity system, garment graphics, hang tags and a 2024 campaign shot across Rotterdam. The lookbook became the foundation of GATEKEEPERS MAG Issue 01.

Where it lives

On backs, walls and screens. Soul Terror is our in-house label and a working test lab: if a system cannot survive a hoodie, a poster and a phone, it is not finished.

Shipped · 2024–2026 · In-house
Case 02 / Product development

Dynamic Fight Gear

Fashion 3D Production art

The brief

Take a martial arts gym from plain training kit to a range its members would actually wear outside the mat. Maintain the school's existing colours and crest.

What we made

3D garment development, placement prints and colourways for rashguards and sleeves. Files prepared for production, not just for a render.

Where it lives

Pre-production. The artwork is ready; the range is being staged for its first run. We show it as work in progress, not a shipped campaign.

In development · 2026
Case 03 / Graphic identity

Marks & Mayhem

In-house Wordmarks Merch Campaign graphics

The brief

Make marks that survive scale. A wordmark, a back print and a campaign graphic all have to read on a hoodie, a sticker and a 9:16 screen.

What we made

Logo and wordmark variations, type treatments, campaign lockups and merch graphics. Built as a small, usable kit instead of a one-off piece of art.

Where it lives

Across Soul Terror apparel and GATEKEEPERS MAG. The same marks are reused at different sizes, so recognition compounds instead of resetting.

Shipped · 2024–2026 · In-house
Case 04 / Publishing arm

Gatekeepers Mag

In-house Editorial Art direction Issue 01

The brief

Give the Soul Terror 2024 lookbook a second life. Turn a shoot into an editorial object with a point of view, not a folder of images with a title slide.

What we made

GATEKEEPERS MAG: a print-first publication about access, taste and the people who build their own rooms. Issue 01 is the Soul Terror lookbook, edited and art-directed as a magazine.

Where it lives

Print and digital. The magazine is also the template for a client offer: editorial direction, layout and production for brands with a story worth printing.
