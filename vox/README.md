## Voice rules (non-negotiable)

- Street, punk, cultured. Short sentences. No corporate fluff. No AI tells.
- Copy bank phrases live in `docs/BRAND.md`. Use them; do not invent new taglines.
- AI stays invisible. Posts read as human-made.
- Label in-house work as in-house (Soul Terror, Dynamic Martial Arts, SLTR Radio).
- FRNTHASIA and VANDALACTISM are independent friends, not Haki-owned.
- Dynamic Fight Gear is in development, not shipped.
- Never present in-house work as client work.
- Never invent metrics, followers, case studies, testimonials or results.

## Cadence (Instagram, starting point)

- Feed posts: 3–4 per week (mix of carousel, single image, reel).
- Stories: daily when there is something real to show. Silence beats filler.
- Reels: 1–2 per week, process and POV content.
- No posting just to hit a number.
