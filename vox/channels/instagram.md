# Instagram — @producedbyhaki

**Status:** active. **Owner:** VOX. **Taste sign-off:** The Eye.

## The account

- Handle: @producedbyhaki
- Role in the funnel: proof + POV + people. The top of the funnel and the trust layer. Every profile links to hakiproductions.com (once live).
- Audience: starting streetwear labels, artists/DJs, gyms and fight schools, event organisers, small brands rebranding. Rotterdam-first, NL-wide, international artists only.

## Cadence

| Format | Frequency | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Carousel | 2/week | Proof and process. Saves and shares. |
| Reel | 1–2/week | Reach. POV and behind-the-scenes. |
| Single image | 1/week | Offers and availability. |
| Story | daily when real | Behind-the-scenes, polls, replies. Silence beats filler. |

Total feed: 3–4 posts/week. Never post to hit a number.

## Posting windows (Rotterdam, CET)

- Mon–Fri: 18:00–20:00 (after work, before dinner).
- Sat: 11:00–13:00.
- Sun: 17:00–19:00.
Test and adjust per `marketing/06-experiments.md` once we have data. No invented "best times" claims until measured.

## Content pillars (from Audience agent)

1. **Proof** — finished work, campaigns, product, before/after.
2. **Process** — how it was made, decisions, rejects, lessons.
3. **POV** — street, punk, cultured. Strong opinions, not generic advice.
4. **People** — Francisca, the crew, collaborators, the community.
5. **Offers** — clear services, prices, availability, calls to action.

## Format rules

- Carousels: 5–8 slides. Hook on slide 1. One idea per slide. Last slide is the CTA.
- Reels: 7–15 seconds. Hook in the first 2 seconds. Captions on screen. No trending audio that fights the brand.
- Single image: one strong frame + a caption that does the work.
- Stories: raw over polished. Polls and question stickers only when there is a real question.

## Hashtag strategy

Three tiers, max 12 tags per post:

- **Broad (1–2):** #rotterdam #creativeagency
- **Niche (4–6):** #streetwearbrand #lookbookdesign #eventrecap #brandidentity
- **Branded (1–2):** #producedbyhaki #hakiproductions

No 30-tag spam. Rotate tags per post to avoid shadow-ban patterns. Track which tags drive reach in `reports/`.

## What we do not do

- No bought followers, bots, engagement pods, follow-for-follow.
- No generic motivational quotes.
- No stock photography presented as Haki work.
- No AI-generated imagery presented as Haki work.
- No posting client work without written permission.
- No prices hidden. "From €X" qualifies leads.
- No ads on The Press Kit.
