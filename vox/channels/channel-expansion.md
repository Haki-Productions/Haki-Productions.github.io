# Channel expansion — designed, not active

**Status:** designed. **Activation:** needs M0rph's go-ahead and The Eye's taste sign-off per channel.
**Owner:** VOX prepares. M0rph approves.

Instagram is the only active channel today. The channels below are mapped so VOX can move fast when M0rph says go, without inventing strategy on the spot. None of these are live. Do not create accounts or post until approved.

## Decision rule for adding a channel

Add a channel only when all three are true:
1. Instagram cadence is stable (3–4 posts/week, 4 weeks running).
2. There is content to repurpose, not new content to invent.
3. VOX has the weekly time budget. One channel at a time.

Kill a channel after 4 weeks if it produces zero qualified conversations and zero meaningful reach. Log the kill in `reports/`.

## TikTok (secondary, reach + process)

- **Job:** reach and process content. Repurpose, do not duplicate blindly.
- **Cadence:** 3–5/week, 7–15s, vertical.
- **Content:** behind-the-scenes, rejects, "how we made this", POV takes. Raw over polished.
- **Repurpose from IG:** take the strongest IG Reels, re-cut with TikTok-native hooks and captions. Never cross-post identical files.
- **Hashtags:** 3–5, niche + 1 broad.
- **Do not:** expect leads. TikTok is reach, not bookings (per growth playbook).
- **Taste gate:** The Eye signs off on the first 5 videos before posting.

## LinkedIn (B2B, gyms/events/brands)

- **Job:** B2B leads. Gyms, event organisers, fashion brands, rebranding clients.
- **Cadence:** 1–2/week, text + single image or carousel (PDF).
- **Content:** case studies, "how we approached this", pricing transparency, availability. Dutch and English.
- **Voice:** more direct and professional than IG, still human. No corporate fluff.
- **Repurpose from IG:** turn IG carousels into LinkedIn PDF carousels; turn proof posts into short case-study text posts.
- **Do not:** post consumer/streetwear content. Different audience.
- **Taste gate:** The Eye signs off on voice and claims.

## X / Twitter (optional, low priority)

- **Job:** industry presence, sharing work, joining conversations.
- **Cadence:** 3–5/week, short.
- **Content:** work-in-progress, opinions, links to case studies.
- **Decision:** only if M0rph wants a public voice there. Low leverage for Haki today. Skip unless asked.

## Email newsletter (owned audience, highest leverage long-term)

- **Job:** owned channel. No algorithm. Converts warm contacts.
- **Cadence:** monthly or bi-weekly. Short.
- **Content:** one piece of work, one POV, one CTA. Link to toolbox/press kit.
- **Builds from:** `marketing/03-outreach.md` warm-contact list + toolbox lead magnet.
- **Do not:** start until there is a list of 50+ real contacts. Premature newsletter is noise.
- **Taste gate:** The Eye on voice; M0rph on anything client-facing.

## YouTube Shorts (optional)

- **Job:** search + long-tail reach for process content.
- **Cadence:** 1–2/week, repurposed from Reels.
- **Decision:** only after IG Reels are consistent and there is editing capacity. Low priority.
