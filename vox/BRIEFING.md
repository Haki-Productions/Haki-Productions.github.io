## Instagram strategy (from social skill)

**Platform specs:**
- Best for: Visual brands, lifestyle
- Frequency: 1-2 posts + Stories daily
- Key format: Reels, carousels

**Content pillars framework:** Build around 3-5 pillars. Each pillar: unique perspective, audience questions, past performance, consistency, business alignment.

**Hook formulas:** Curiosity, story, value, contrarian. The first line determines whether anyone reads the rest.

**Content repurposing:** Turn one piece into many. Blog → LinkedIn insight + Twitter thread + Instagram carousel + Reel. Podcast → 3-5 clips + LinkedIn post + Twitter thread + carousel.

**Engagement routine (30 min daily):**
1. Respond to all comments (5 min)
2. Comment on 5-10 target accounts (15 min)
3. Share/repost with added insight (5 min)
4. Send 2-3 DMs to new connections (5 min)

**Analytics that matter:** Impressions, reach, follower growth rate, engagement rate, comments (higher value than likes), shares/reposts, saves, link clicks, profile visits, DMs received.

## Content strategy (from content-strategy skill)

**Content is brand surface area.** Each published piece is a new entry point. Hundreds of pieces compound into hundreds of doorways working 24/7.

**Calendar split: 60/30/10**
- 60% searchable (use-case content, how-tos)
- 30% shareable (thought leadership, original data, opinion)
- 10% experimental (new formats, channels, bets)

**Per-format discipline:**
- Blog post: 10 title options, ~5 editing passes
- Video: script the hook first, front-load the payoff
- Email: one idea per send, subject line is the title

**Create Once, Distribute Twice:** one exceptional piece, reformatted across every channel. Distribution hooks built in at creation time.

## Trend research (from eye-trends)

**Horizon model:**
- Now (0-6 months): already visible. Act on it.
- Next (6-18 months): credible early signals. Bet small.
- Noise (fading): peaked, saturated. Avoid or subvert.

**Evidence grades:** A (primary/first-hand), B (editorial with POV), C (aggregator, directional only).

**Signal test:** What changed in behaviour? Who adopted first? Growing, plateauing or parodying? What does it mean for Haki's offers? What would prove this wrong? Look, tool or value shift?

**Six scan lanes:** Fashion/streetwear, Music/nightlife, Design/type, Tech/internet, Sport/fight culture, Local/Rotterdam.

**Rules:** Date-stamp everything. Separate observation from interpretation ([fact] vs [interpretation]). No single-platform algorithm as proof. Kill your darlings.
