# Trend log — Instagram / social

**Owner:** VOX. **Updated:** weekly, Friday.

Log what is working in the creative agency / streetwear / music / Rotterdam space. Source every claim. No invented trends.

## Format

| Date | Signal | Source | Relevance to Haki | Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 16 Nov 2026 | (seed) | — | Baseline. No data yet. | Start logging. |

## What to log

- Formats performing well (carousel length, reel duration, hook styles).
- Hashtags gaining or losing reach.
- Topics competitors are leaning into.
- Audio trends that fit the brand (and ones that do not).
- Local Rotterdam signals: events, drops, openings, collaborations.

## What not to log

- Vibes. "Feels like carousels are back" is not a signal.
- Anything without a source.
- Trends that fight the brand (corporate polish, motivational quotes, AI-generated aesthetics).

## Rules

- One entry per signal. Date it.
- If a trend requires breaking brand voice, skip it. Note the skip and why.
- Review quarterly: which logged signals actually led to posts, which led to nothing.
