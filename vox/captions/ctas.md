# CTAs — Instagram

One CTA per post. Last slide of a carousel, last line of a caption. Match the CTA to the pillar.

## Proof posts
- "DM for a lookbook."
- "Building this with them. More soon."
- "Book a recap."
- "Which one hits hardest?"

## Process posts
- "Save this for your next drop."
- "Follow for more."
- "Follow the process."

## POV posts
- "Agree or fight me."
- "Agree or disagree. Tell us."
- "Save this. Send it to the friend who keeps asking for just a logo."
- "DM if this lands."

## People posts
- "Meet the crew."

## Offer posts
- "Link in bio."
- "DM 'PRESS'."
- "DM 'ENGINE'."
- "DM 'LAUNCH'."

## Rules
- One CTA per post. Two CTAs is no CTA.
- Offer CTAs always point to a real, working path. No dead links.
- Never "Click the link in our bio to learn more about our services" (corporate). Short or nothing.
- If there is no honest CTA, end with a question instead.
