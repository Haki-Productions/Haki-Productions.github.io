# Hooks — Instagram

Short openers for slides 1, Reel frame 1, and story covers. One line. Test against the 2-second rule: if it does not earn the next second, cut it.

## POV hooks
- What makes a brand impossible to ignore.
- Streetwear brands don't need more content.
- Stop asking for a logo. Ask for a world.
- Why we don't do free pitches.
- A logo is a signature. A world is a reason to stay.

## People hooks

## Offer hooks

- The Engine. €1,950 per month.
- The Launch. From €2,500.

## Rules
- Hook is the whole post. If the hook is weak, the post is dead.
- No clickbait that the caption does not pay off.
- Never use "You won't believe..." or "This changed everything."
