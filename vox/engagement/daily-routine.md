# Daily engagement routine — Instagram

**Time budget:** 20–30 minutes per day. No more. Engagement is a habit, not a job.

## Daily (Mon–Sat, ~20 min)

1. **Reply (5 min).** Reply to every comment on posts from the last 7 days. Reply to DMs. If a DM is a lead, hand to M0rph, do not close it yourself.
2. **Outbound (10 min).** Engage genuinely with 5–10 accounts in the target segments: streetwear labels, artists/DJs, gyms/fight schools, event organisers, small brands. Leave real comments. No "🔥🔥🔥". No follow-for-follow.
3. **Story (5 min).** One story if there is something real. Poll or question sticker only with a real question. Silence beats filler.

## Weekly (Friday, ~30 min)

1. Review the week's posts against `reports/weekly-template.md`.
2. Log what worked, what did not, one hypothesis for next week.
3. Update `trends/trend-log.md` if anything shifted.
4. Plan next week's batch for The Eye review.

## Reply templates (human voice)

Use as starting points. Rewrite every time. Never paste verbatim twice.

- **Compliment on work:** "Thanks. Took longer than it should have. Worth it."
- **Question about service:** "DM me 'PRESS' / 'ENGINE' / 'LAUNCH' and I'll send the details."
- **Price question:** "From €X. Depends on scope. DM and I'll ask three questions."
- **Collab request:** "Send me what you're building and a deadline. I'll come back to you."
- **Generic "nice":** "Appreciate it." (Short. Move on.)

## Do not

- Do not auto-reply. Do not use bots.
- Do not engage with accounts that are clearly bots or engagement pods.
- Do not argue in comments. Take it to DM or let it go.
- Do not post from a personal account pretending to be Haki.
- Do not buy followers, use follow/unfollow, or run engagement groups.
