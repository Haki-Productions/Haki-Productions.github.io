STREET, PUNK AND cultured.

Haki Productions started in the same places we still work: the street, the studio, the venue, the group chat where the next idea lands. Rotterdam is the base. The outlook is international.

One crew, five fronts. Content and media. Fashion and merchandise. Music. Copy and publishing. Event media. That range is not a menu of random services. It is how we keep a brand coherent from the first sketch to the final post.

The crew is small on purpose. Core team, trusted collaborators, no layers of account managers between you and the work. You talk to the people making it. That is the whole point.

You keep the vision. We handle the details.
