THE honest ANSWERS.

If your question is not here, ask it directly. We answer email within two working days.

How fast can you start?

Most projects can start within two to three weeks. Smaller jobs, like a press kit, can often move faster. If you have a hard deadline, say it in the first email and we will tell you straight away whether it is realistic.

What do you need from me?

A clear goal, access to your existing brand material, and one person who can approve work. That is the minimum. References help. A 40-page brief is not required.

Do you work outside Rotterdam?

Yes. We are based in Rotterdam and work across the Netherlands and internationally. Travel and accommodation are quoted separately and agreed before booking.

How does payment work?

Projects are split 50% deposit and 50% on delivery. Retainers are invoiced monthly in advance. Invoices are paid within 14 days. Final files are released once the outstanding balance is settled. Prices exclude VAT where applicable.

What is not included?

Extra shoot days, additional cutdowns, extra revision rounds, the Press Kit web page, rush delivery, travel outside Rotterdam, location hire, permits, talent fees, music licences and stock licences are quoted separately. You will see them in the proposal before anything starts.

What if we need more revisions?

Each offer includes a fixed number of rounds: one per asset on The Engine, one on The Press Kit and The Recap, two on The Launch and The Identity. Extra rounds are quoted before they start. We will tell you when a request falls outside the agreed scope.

Can we change the scope?

Yes, before the work starts or with a written change order. A new deliverable, a new location or a new deadline can change the price and the schedule. We quote the difference first, then continue.

Why does this cost more than a freelancer?

A freelancer can be cheaper for one clip and one camera. You are hiring a crew, a producer, a plan and a finished set of assets that work together. The quote covers the shoot, the edit, the revisions and the people who make the work. If you only need one person for one shot, say so and we will tell you honestly if a freelancer is the better buy.

Can you handle a bigger production?

Yes, with the right budget. Fashion production, music projects, publishing and full event activation are scoped on request. We bring in trusted collaborators rather than pretending one person can do everything.

Is this AI-generated work?

No synthetic faces, no fake campaigns, no generated case studies. Tools may help us move faster behind the scenes, but the direction, the shoots and the final craft are human. If that ever changes for a specific deliverable, we will say so before you commission it.
