# Pipelines — The Tiger Lead Tracker

**Owner:** The Tiger  
**Purpose:** Track every lead from first touch through close, using The Tiger's qualification framework and stage system.

---

## 1. Pipeline stages

Every lead progresses through these stages. No lead sits without a next action for more than 48 hours.

| Stage | Description | Next action required |
|---|---|---|
| **New** | Lead enters the system (form, email, DM, referrals) | Record lead source & date; assign first touch |
| **Qualified** | Budget ≥ €750, real deadline, decision-maker reachable, need matches Haki's offers | Apply the **four-axis sniff test** (budget, timeline, authority, need) |
| **Contacted** | First meaningful contact made (email, call, Cal.com intro) | Log date, method, and summary of conversation |
| **Call booked** | Cal.com intro call scheduled | Send calendar invite; prep offer mapping |
| **Proposal sent** | Formal proposal/quote delivered | Log proposal date, offer type, and value |
| **Negotiating** | Prospect asks questions, requests changes, or discusses terms | Track objection type; apply objection handling |
| **Closed (won)** | Deposit paid (50%) or retainer signed | Record deal value, deposit date, and onboarding steps |
| **Closed (lost)** | Prospect declines, goes quiet, or says no after proposal | Archive after 3 touchpoints with no reply |

---

## 2. The Tiger's four-axis sniff test (qualification)

A lead is **qualified** only if it passes all four axes. If it fails two or more, drop it — no exceptions.

| Axis | Question | Pass criteria |
|---|---|---|
| **Budget** | Do they have €750+? | Yes → continue. No → point to Toolbox, archive. |
| **Timeline** | Is there a real deadline? "Someday" is not a project. | Specific date or event (launch, festival, rebrand). Continue. |
| **Authority** | Are you talking to the decision-maker? | Yes, or clear path to decision-maker. Continue. |
| **Need** | Does the problem Haki solve match what they need? | Their trigger aligns with one of Haki's five offers. Continue. |

**Lead scoring:** Qualified = passes all 4. Fails 2+ → drop. Fails 1 → re-evaluate or refer.

## 6. Objection handling (The Tiger's responses)

**The four objections and exact responses:**

| Objection | Tiger's response |
|---|---|
| **"Too expensive."** → Scope down. *"What is the one thing you need most?"* Offer the smallest project that solves it. |
| **"I need to think about it."** → *"What is the specific thing you are unsure about?"* Get the real objection out. |
| **"Can you do it cheaper?"** → *"I can reduce scope, not quality. Here is what we cut."* Never discount without cutting scope. |
| **"I found someone cheaper."** → *"Good. Compare what they deliver for that price. If they do the same thing for less, take it. If they do less, you are not comparing the same thing."* |

**Never discount without reducing scope.** The price reflects the promise.

---

## 7. Pipeline hygiene rules

- No lead sits without a next action for more than 48 hours.
- A lead that does not reply after 3 touches gets archived. Do not chase ghosts.
- Update the pipeline stage and next action every time you touch a lead.
- Archive closed (won or lost) leads at month end, but keep the row for history.
- Pipeline review: Every Monday, review all active leads and confirm next actions.
