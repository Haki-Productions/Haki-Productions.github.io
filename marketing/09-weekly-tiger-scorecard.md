## 2. Calculation: The Tiger's math

| Step | Formula | This week | Required to hit month target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Conversations → Replies | Replies ÷ Conversations | | ≥ 40% reply rate |
| Replies → Calls booked | Calls booked ÷ Replies | | ≥ 50% booking rate |
| Calls booked → Proposals sent | Proposals sent ÷ Calls booked | | ≥ 100% (every call leads to a proposal) |
| Proposals sent → Closed (won) | Closed ÷ Proposals sent | | ≥ 20% close rate (per The Tiger's math: 2 closes per 10 proposals) |

**Month one milestone:** One paid project + one retainer.

## 4. Red flags (stop and investigate)

| Condition | Action |
|---|---|
| Replies < 40% of conversations started | Review conversation quality and targeting. |
| Calls booked < 50% of replies | Review Cal.com intro flow and lead qualification. |
| Proposals sent < 100% of calls booked | Review proposal delivery and follow-up timing. |
| Close rate < 20% of proposals sent | Review offer-market fit, pricing, or proof on site. |
| No deposits after 2 weeks of proposals | Review objection handling and proposal clarity. |
