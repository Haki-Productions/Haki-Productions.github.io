# PRICING — Haki Productions

_Last updated: 15 November 2026 — v2, lowered for the Dutch market_

## 1. Decision

The first correction moved the prices from below cost to a price above cost. That
was right on the cost maths and wrong on the market. Dutch small brands and
artists are price-sensitive, and the first set read as agency pricing to buyers
who compare against freelancers.

This version lowers every public price by 10–25% and trims the scope to match.
The work still covers a paid crew and a real edit. It is not a return to the
old prices, which were below cost.

| Offer | Original | Previous | **Now** | Scope now |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| **The Engine** | €600/mo | €2,400/mo | **€1,950/mo** | One shoot day, 6 verticals, 1 photo set, captions. Three-month minimum. |
| **The Launch** | €750 | from €3,000 | **from €2,500** | One production day, 1 hero film, 5 verticals, 8 photos, launch copy. |
| **The Identity** | €900 | from €3,200 | **from €2,400** | Logo suite, colour, type, brand guide, 5 social templates. Three weeks. |
| **The Press Kit** | €350 | €950 | **€750** | Bio, 5 edited existing photos, cover art, press highlights, PDF. Web page +€250. |
| **The Recap** | €500 | from €2,000 | **from €1,800** | Up to 3 hours, recap film, 6 verticals, 15 photos. |

**Public floor:** projects from **€750**. Retainers from **€1,950 per month**.
**Payment:** 50% deposit, 50% on delivery. Retainers monthly in advance.
**Revisions:** The Engine 1 round per asset; Launch and Identity 2 rounds; Press
Kit and Recap 1 round. Extra round €125.
**VAT:** prices exclude VAT where applicable.
**Founding clients:** 10% off the first project or first three retainer months,
in exchange for a testimonial and permission to publish a case study. This is a
sales decision, not a public price.

## 2. Why the first correction was too high

The previous set was defensible against published agency benchmarks. It was
still the wrong number for Haki's actual buyer.

- **The buyer is not an established SME.** A Rotterdam artist, a starting
  streetwear label or a local venue compares €2,400/month to a freelancer at
  €500–€800, not to an agency retainer at €5,000. The benchmark was real; the
  comparison set was wrong.
- **The Dutch market negotiates hard on visible price.** A high list price does
  not create perceived value here. It creates a reason not to reply. Lower
  entry prices get the conversation; the scope and the proposal carry the
  value.
- **The old prices were still below cost.** This is not a licence to go back.
  At €600/month The Engine could not pay a shooter, an editor and a photo
  editor. The new floor stays above the lean cost model.
- **Lower price needs a smaller promise.** Keeping eight videos, two photo sets
  and a four-hour event at the lower number would rebuild the old loss. The
  scope is trimmed with the price.

## 4. Cost model used for the new prices

These are planning assumptions for a lean Rotterdam crew, not payroll. Replace
them with the real day rates before signing a large project.

| Input | Assumption |
|---|---:|
| Shooter, one day | €400 |
| Second crew member, one day | €250 |
| Editor, one day | €300 |
| Photo editor, one day | €300 |
| Designer, one day | €400 |
| Copy or strategy, one day | €400 |
| Project management | 10% of direct cost |
| Contingency | 10% of direct cost |

| Offer | Direct cost (incl. PM and contingency) | Price | Gross margin |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| The Engine | ~€1,620 | €1,950 | ~17% |
| The Launch | ~€2,100 | €2,500 | ~16% |
| The Identity | ~€1,920 | €2,400 | ~20% |
| The Press Kit | ~€660 | €750 | ~12% |
| The Recap | ~€1,560 | €1,800 | ~13% |

The scope assumptions behind those costs:

- **The Engine:** one shooter, two edit days for six verticals, half a day of
  photo editing, half a day of planning and captions. No second crew on a
  standard content day.
- **The Launch:** shooter plus second crew, two edit days, one photo edit day,
  half a day of launch copy.
- **The Identity:** three designer days plus one strategy/copy day.
- **The Press Kit:** half a day of copy, half a day of design, half a day of
  photo editing. The web page is not included at €750.
- **The Recap:** one shooter for up to three hours, two edit days, one photo
  edit day. A second shooter or a longer event needs the add-on.

These margins are before sales, admin, software, insurance, equipment and
founders' profit. They are a floor, not a promise. If the real crew cost is
higher, raise the price or cut the scope. Do not absorb it.

## 5. Pricing rules

### What is included

- The deliverables named on the offer card.
- The stated number of revision rounds.
- One production day where the offer says so.
- Delivery in the stated format and timeline.
- Final exports. Source files are included where the offer says so.
- Commercial usage of the delivered work, unless a third-party licence says
  otherwise.

### What is not included

- Extra shoot days: €650 per day.
- Extra vertical cutdown: €100 each.
- Extra revision round: €125.
- Rush delivery under five working days: +25%.
- Extra event hour: €150.
- Web page version of the Press Kit: €250.
- Travel outside Rotterdam: €0.45/km or quoted before booking.
- Accommodation, permits, location hire, talent fees, music licences and stock
  licences: quoted separately.
- VAT, where applicable.

### Payment

- Projects: 50% deposit to book the dates, 50% on delivery.
- Retainers: monthly in advance, three-month minimum, 30 days' notice after.
- Invoices: 14 days.
- Final files are released when the outstanding balance is paid.

### Scope changes

A change to the deliverables, timeline or number of revisions is quoted in
writing before the extra work starts. No verbal scope changes.

### Discounts

- Founding clients: 10% off the first project or first three retainer months,
  in exchange for a testimonial and permission to publish a case study.
- No permanent discount. No discount without a reason.
- Payment plans are available for projects over €1,500. They do not change the
  price.

## 6. What this means for the plan

The operating target stays €4,000/month. At the lower prices it takes one
retainer plus one project, not two retainers alone:

| Route | Mix | Revenue |
|---|---|---:|
| Retainers only | 2 × The Engine | €3,900/mo |
| Retainer + project | 1 × The Engine + 1 × The Launch | €4,450 |
| Retainer + identity | 1 × The Engine + 1 × The Identity | €4,350 |
| Project route | 1 × The Identity + 1 × The Launch | €4,900 |
| Event route | 1 × The Recap + 1 × The Engine | €3,750/mo |
| Lean month | 2 × The Press Kit + 1 × The Recap | €3,300 |

Two retainers alone land just under target. A third small project or a Press
Kit closes the gap. The €1,000/month figure stays as an emergency floor, not as
the plan.

## 7. Open assumptions to validate

1. **Actual crew day rates.** The cost model uses lean assumptions. Confirm what
   the core crew and regular collaborators charge.
2. **Demand at the lower prices.** Track the first ten proposals: how many
   prospects accept, negotiate or disappear. If the close rate is still weak,
   the problem may be the offer or the proof, not the price.
3. **Press Kit margin.** At €750 the kit is a 12% entry offer. If it sells but
   stays under 10%, raise it to €850 or keep the web page as a paid add-on.
4. **Recap scope.** Confirm that one shooter covers a three-hour event and that
   clients accept €150 per extra hour and the 25% rush fee.
5. **VAT and KvK.** Confirm the invoicing setup with an accountant before taking
   deposits.
6. **Usage rights.** Confirm that the standard terms allow Haki to show the work
   in its portfolio, and that third-party music and stock are licensed for the
   client's use.
7. **Founding-client discount.** Cap it at three clients and put the exchange
   (testimonial + case study) in writing.

## 8. Review trigger

Review the prices after one of these happens:

- Ten proposals have been sent at the lower prices.
- Three projects have been delivered.
- The real crew cost is known.
- A client asks for a scope that does not fit the card.

Do not change the public price in a quiet edit. Update the site, the plan, the
proposal template and the invoice description together.

## 9. The floor

This is the lowest set that still pays a crew under the lean model. Going lower
means one of three things:

1. Cut the scope again (fewer videos, fewer photos, shorter coverage).
2. Do the work yourself and accept a lower effective day rate.
3. Sell a smaller entry offer, such as the Press Kit, and upsell from there.

Do not cut the price alone. A lower number with the same promise is how the
business ends up busy and broke.
