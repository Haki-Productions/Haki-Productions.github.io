# HAKI PRODUCTIONS — Working Plan
_Last updated: 15 November 2026 — v2, prices lowered for the Dutch market_

## 1. The goal

- **Operating target:** €4,000/month to start. The old €1,000/month figure is the emergency floor, not the plan.
- **Why:** the prices cover a crew day, a real edit and a margin without pricing Haki out of the Dutch small-brand market. See `PRICING.md` for the evidence and the cost model.
- **Scale rule:** reinvest and add offers only when demand is proven.
- **Core focus:** the agency website. It must sell what Haki can do for clients.
- **Other divisions** (fashion production, music, publishing, full event activation) are **on request only** — and only when there is a real budget.

## 2. The money math

€4,000/month is one of these:

| Path | Mix | Revenue |
|---|---|---:|

| Retainer + project | 1 × The Engine + 1 × The Launch | €4,450 |

| Project route | 1 × The Identity + 1 × The Launch | €4,900 |

**Sales target:** 20 conversations → 5 proposals → 2 closes.
**Month-one milestone:** one paid project + one retainer.

## 3. Core offers (v2, 15 November 2026)

| Offer | Deliverables | Price |
|---|---|---|
| **The Engine** | One shoot day + 6 vertical videos + 1 photo set + captions per month | €1,950/mo |
| **The Launch** | 1 hero film + 5 verticals + 8 photos + copy | from €2,500 |
| **The Identity** | Logo, colour, type, brand guide, 5 social templates | from €2,400 |
| **The Press Kit** | Bio, 5 existing photos edited, cover art, press highlights, PDF (web page +€250) | €750 |
| **The Recap** | Up to 3 hours event coverage + recap film + 6 verticals + 15 photos | from €1,800 |

**Minimum project:** €750. **Retainers:** from €1,950/month.
Everything else: "Full capabilities on request."

### Pricing rules

- **Deposit:** 50% to book, 50% on delivery. Retainers monthly in advance.
- **Revisions:** 1 round per asset on The Engine; 1 on The Press Kit and The Recap; 2 on The Launch and The Identity. Extra round €125.
- **Add-ons:** extra shoot day €650; extra vertical €100; extra event hour €150; Press Kit web page €250; rush delivery under five working days +25%; travel €0.45/km or quoted.
- **Not included:** location hire, permits, talent fees, music and stock licences, accommodation, VAT where applicable.
- **Founding clients:** 10% off the first project or first three retainer months, in exchange for a testimonial and a published case study. Cap at three clients.
- **Scope changes:** quoted in writing before the extra work starts.

## 4. Site blueprint

Five pages, one job: turn attention into booked calls.

1. **Home** — positioning, proof, core offers, process, CTA.
2. **Services** — the five offers, deliverables, timelines, starting prices, budget qualifier.
3. **Work** — 4–6 case studies: Soul Terror, FRNTHASIA / VANDALACTISM, identity work, event coverage.
4. **About** — Haki story, Francisca, the crew, the ethos.
5. **Contact** — service + budget + deadline form, then Cal.com booking.

**Lead magnet:** "The Artist Press Kit Checklist" — email capture that feeds the press kit offer.

**SEO:** Rotterdam + service keywords ("creative agency Rotterdam", "streetwear brand design", "event content agency"), Google Business Profile, consistent NAP.

## 7. 30-day launch sequence

### Week 1 — build
- Lock copy and offer prices.
- Build home + services + contact.
- Add proof assets (Soul Terror, FRNTHASIA, identity work).
- Set up local preview.

### Week 2 — launch
- Register domain, deploy, DNS.
- Google Business Profile + Instagram launch post.
- Publish 3 case studies and the press kit checklist.
- Set up booking + inquiry form.

### Week 3 — sell
- 20 warm conversations (Francisca's network + Haki contacts).
- Send 5 tailored proposals.
- Founding-client offer: 10% off the first 3 clients in exchange for a testimonial + case study. Put the exchange in writing.

### Week 4 — close + deliver
- Close 1–2 projects.
- Collect 50% deposits.
- Deliver fast, document the process, ask for referrals.

## 8. Marketing engine

- **Francisca's lane:** word-of-mouth, community, online promotion. Her labels are proof and distribution.
- **Content:** process breakdowns, before/after, client wins, behind-the-scenes. Post where the audience already is (Instagram/TikTok).
- **Outreach:** 20 warm contacts per week; no cold spam.
- **Paid ads:** only after the site converts organically. Start at €0; test €100–150/month later.
- **Referrals:** ask every client at delivery; offer a 10% credit.

## 9. Finance flow (legitimate + simple)

1. Intro call → written scope + price.
2. 50% deposit before work starts.
3. Delivery → final 50%.
4. Invoice + receipt for every payment.
5. Use Mollie or Stripe; keep business money separate.
6. Confirm Dutch KvK/BTW requirements with an accountant.

M0rpheus can map the flow and draft the templates. The accounts must stay in the owners' names.

## 10. Scale path

1. **€4k/month:** 1 retainer + 1 project, or 2 projects, or 2 retainers + 1 Press Kit.
2. **€6–8k/month:** 3 retainers + project overflow; raise prices 10–15% after three delivered case studies.
3. **€10k+/month:** productized offers, subcontractors, press kit generator, Soul Terror store, VANDALACTISM merch.
4. **Beyond:** Gatekeepers Mag sponsorships, SLTRTV, event IP (SOULNADO, Sol et Terra).
