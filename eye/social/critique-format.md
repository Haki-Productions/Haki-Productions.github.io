# The Eye — critique format for social drafts

**Used by:** The Eye reviewing VOX draft batches.
**Read by:** VOX before submitting, M0rph when approving.

## Submission format (VOX → The Eye)

VOX submits a batch of 4–6 posts as a single message containing:
- Post IDs (from `vox/calendar/instagram-30d.md`)
- Caption text (from `vox/captions/caption-bank.md`)
- Asset references
- The hook and CTA for each

## Critique format (The Eye → VOX)

For each post in the batch:

```
ID: IG-XX
Verdict: APPROVE | REVISE | REJECT
Reason (one sentence):
Fix (if REVISE/REJECT, concrete, not vague):
```

### Verdicts

- **APPROVE.** Ready for M0rph's publish approval. Log in sign-off log.
- **REVISE.** Specific fix required. VOX revises and re-submits. One round of revision per batch unless The Eye says otherwise.
- **REJECT.** Does not meet the rubric. VOX rewrites from scratch or drops it.

### Rules for The Eye

- Feedback is concrete. "Cut this line" beats "feels off".
- Feedback is about taste, not metrics. Do not ask "will this get likes".
- Do not rewrite the caption. Point at the problem and let VOX fix it.
- One round of revision per batch. If it is still wrong after that, reject.
- Log every verdict in `signoff-log.md`, including approvals.

### Rules for VOX

- Submit batches, not single posts. The Eye reviews in batches.
- Do not submit a post The Eye has already rejected in a new batch without changing it.
- Do not schedule anything without an APPROVE logged in `signoff-log.md`.
- Do not argue with a verdict. Revise or drop.
