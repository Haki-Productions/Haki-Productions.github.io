# The Eye — taste rubric for social content

**Owner:** The Eye. **Read by:** VOX before drafting, M0rph before approving.

Social content is judged on the same taste standards as everything else Haki makes. Street, punk, cultured. Short sentences. No corporate fluff. No AI tells. AI stays invisible.

## The five questions

Every draft is judged against these. A draft must pass all five to move forward.

1. **Does it read as human?** If a reader could tell an AI wrote it, it fails. No "it's not X, it's Y" reveals, no forced triads, no one-line closers, no trailing comma clauses.
2. **Is it on-voice?** Does it sound like Haki, not like a generic agency? Check against `docs/BRAND.md` copy bank. New taglines are not allowed.
3. **Is the proof honest?** In-house work labeled as in-house. No invented metrics. No client work without permission. Dynamic Fight Gear is "in development".
4. **Does it earn the scroll-stop?** The hook must earn the next second. If the first line is weak, the post is dead regardless of the rest.
5. **Is there one idea?** One post, one idea, one CTA. If there are two, split them.

## What fails automatically

- AI tells of any kind (see humanizer skill).
- Generic motivational quotes.
- Stock photography presented as Haki work.
- AI-generated imagery presented as Haki work.
- Hidden prices on offer posts.
- In-house work framed as client work.
- FRNTHASIA or VANDALACTISM described as Haki-owned.
- Dynamic Fight Gear described as shipped.
- Engagement bait with no substance ("tag a friend who...").

## Taste is not popularity

A post can perform well and still be off-brand. A post can be on-brand and perform poorly. The Eye judges taste, not metrics. Metrics are VOX's job in `vox/reports/`.
