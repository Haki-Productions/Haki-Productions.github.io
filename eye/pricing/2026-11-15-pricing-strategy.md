# Haki Productions — Pricing Strategy

**Date:** 15 November 2026
**Scope:** The five site offers (Engine, Launch, Identity, Press Kit, Recap), the retainer ladder, talent fees and terms.
**Status:** Recommendation. Nothing on the site is changed yet.
**One-line answer:** The current prices are not low prices. They are below cost. Every offer needs to roughly triple, and two of them need a scope cut to survive.

## 2. The two cost floors

Every price has to clear two different floors. Confusing them is how agencies die slowly.

**Floor A — cash floor.** What actually leaves the bank: freelancers, gear rental, software, travel. At €600/month The Engine clears this. That is why the current prices feel survivable.

**Floor B — full floor.** Cash plus the hours of the people doing the work, priced at what those hours must earn. This is the floor that pays a salary, buys the next lens and survives a slow month.

**The site prices must sit above Floor B.** Floor A is not a business, it is a hobby with invoices.

## 5. The constraint that changes everything

At 45% gross margin, the honest prices are €3,000–€5,000 per project. Those are real agency prices and the market supports them — for an agency with case studies.

Haki does not have those yet. So there are only two moves:

1. **Price at list and accept a thin margin while building proof.** 25–35% gross margin instead of 45–55%. Survivable, not comfortable.
2. **Cut scope so the price is honest at a lower number.** Fewer deliverables, better margin, easier first yes.

The recommendation below does both: list prices land at 25–35% gross margin now, and a scope-trimmed entry rung exists for the first clients. As case studies land, the same scope moves up the list price without a scope change.

### Scope changes that make these prices work

**The Engine — €2,400/mo**
- 4 vertical videos + 1 photo set (12 images) per month, not 8 + 2
- 1 shoot day per month, scheduled in advance
- Add-ons: +4 videos €700, +1 photo set €400
- 3-month minimum stays
- Reason: 8 videos + 2 sets is a €4,000+ retainer. Selling it at €2,400 means working for free every month.

**The Launch — €3,900**
- One production day stays
- 1 hero film, 6 cutdowns, 10 photos stays
- 2 revision rounds stays
- Add: usage terms and a second-day rate

**The Identity — €2,950**
- Logo suite, palette, type, guide, 6 templates stays
- Hard cap: 2 revision rounds, then hourly
- Add: brand guide as a web page, not only PDF (costs nothing, reads as more)

**The Press Kit — €950**
- Bio + 8 edited photos + PDF + web page stays
- Must include a photo session, otherwise it competes with free tools
- Add: a "send-ready" pack with a short pitch email template

**The Recap — €1,950**
- 4h coverage stays
- 1 recap film, 10 cutdowns, 20 photos stays
- Add: 48h express delivery at +30%, additional hour €250

### The entry rung

One new offer, positioned as the way in:

**The Content Day — €1,450**

Cost: 26h × €45 = €1,170 + €120 = €1,290. Margin 11%. It is a door-opener priced to convert, not to profit. It exists to create the case study that unlocks the Engine.

## 7. Talent: pass-through, never absorbed

Clients bring talent, or Haki casts. Either way, talent is a separate line.

**The rule:**
- Talent fee is quoted at cost, visible on the invoice as its own line.
- Haki adds **20% handling** for casting, booking, contracts, call sheets and on-set management.
- Talent is paid **within 14 days of the shoot**, regardless of when the client pays. Haki carries that float and prices it in.
- Usage rights are negotiated per project. A 12-month organic social licence is not the same as paid media, print or billboard. Each tier is priced.
- Exclusivity and buyouts are quoted separately and always in writing.

**Why this matters for the client's talent:** an agency that absorbs talent fees into a package inevitably underpays them or books them for unclear usage. Separating the line is what makes the talent's rate defensible, and it is what makes Haki safe to book through.

**Example, Launch with one model:**
| Line | Amount |
|---|---|
| The Launch | €3,900 |
| Model, 1 day | €650 |
| Casting and handling, 20% | €130 |
| **Client pays** | **€4,680** |
| Haki gross | €3,900 + €130 = €4,030 |

## 8. Terms that protect the price

These go on the services page and in every quote. Without them the listed price is fiction.

| Term | Rule |
|---|---|
| Deposit | 50% to book, 50% on delivery. Retainers paid monthly in advance. |
| Revisions | 2 rounds included per project, 1 per asset in the Engine. Further rounds at €75/h. |
| Scope | Anything not in the written scope is a change order, quoted before work starts. |
| Cancellation | Under 7 days: 50% of the fee. Under 48 hours: 100%. Talent fees are never refundable once booked. |
| Travel | Rotterdam included. Outside Rotterdam at €0.35/km plus travel time at €45/h. |
| Rush | Under 5 working days: +30%. Under 48 hours: quoted on request. |
| Usage | Organic social for 12 months included. Paid media, print, out-of-home and buyouts quoted separately. |
| Credit | Haki may show the work in its portfolio unless the client buys an NDA. |
| Late payment | 14 days, then 2% monthly. |
| BTW | All prices exclude 21% BTW where applicable. |

**Open question for the accountant:** whether Haki is under the KOR (kleineondernemersregeling). Under KOR you charge no BTW but cannot reclaim it on gear, freelancers or software. At €1,000/month turnover the KOR threshold applies, and for a production business that buys a lot of inputs it can cost more than it saves. This changes the effective price by up to 21% and needs a real answer before the site publishes prices.

## 9. The founding client programme

The honest problem: the list price is right, but nobody buys €3,900 from an agency with no client case studies.

The fix is not to lower the list. It is a time-boxed, conditioned discount.

**Founding Client — 20% off list, for the first three clients only.**

Conditions, all in writing:
- Named case study with before/after and real numbers
- Written testimonial
- Permission to use the work in the portfolio and in pitches
- Fixed scope, no extra free work
- Payment terms unchanged

**Why this works:** the discount buys an asset (proof) that is worth more than the 20%. It expires, so it never becomes the real price. And it is honest — the client knows exactly what they are trading.

## 12. Open decisions for Haki

1. **Accept 25–35% gross margin in year one, or cut scope further to hit 45%?** The list above assumes the first.
2. **Is the Content Day a real offer or a founding-client-only deal?** Publishing it permanently sets a low anchor.
3. **KOR or not?** Accountant question, blocking.
4. **Does Haki cast talent, or only work with talent the client brings?** Casting is margin and risk. It is also a reason to be hired.
5. **Is the 20% handling fee acceptable to the talent Haki books?** It should be — it is standard — but it needs to be said out loud in the first conversation, not discovered on the invoice.

## 13. The short version

The current prices are not a strategy. They are a discount applied before anyone asked for one.

- Every offer is 3–8× below the Dutch market for its scope.
- Every offer is below cost at the hours the work actually takes.
- The fix is a 3–4× price rise, one scope cut on the Engine, one new entry offer, and terms that hold the price.
- Talent becomes a visible pass-through line, not something squeezed out of the package.
- The founding-client discount buys proof instead of giving away margin.

The site can carry these prices. The brand is strong enough. What it cannot carry is another year of €600 retainers.
